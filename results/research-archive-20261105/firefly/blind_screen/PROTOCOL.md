# Prospective, conditional FIREFLY screen of the 72-hour archive
This local pre-outcome protocol and candidate lock are hashed before dispatch. The investigators have previously seen the baseline and two OMP designs. No novel-candidate FIREFLY result is used for selection. This is not a public preregistration.
1. Eligible logged records have original feasible=true, physics_evaluated=true, cached=false and a finite positive objective. Deduplicate by SHA256 identity then by coefficients rounded to 1 mm (IMP junction fraction to 0.01). Choose the deterministic hash-minimum representative irrespective of score. Separate strata OMP20, OMP50, OMP100, IMP20 and IMP50. IMP100 has zero heat evaluations. Equal weight per eligible stratum. Random samples this adaptive archive, not the entire design space.
2. Before ranking, traverse a rank-blind reproducible SHA256 permutation of representatives in each stratum. Form a 60-shape candidate panel per stratum. Apply continuous engineering constraints, canonical complete-wall 0.1 micrometre geometry screening and archived feasible length constraint. Exclude material walls within 1 mm discrete Hausdorff distance of baseline or either already-tested candidate. Exclude geometries within 1 mm maximum corresponding material-point displacement of an earlier panel entry (641 points per active surface). Draw at most 1200 per stratum. If fewer than 25 qualify, report the stratum as untestable; otherwise use the attained panel. Archive fractions and exclusions are recorded. Panel quantiles describe eligible panel, not exact archive quintiles.
3. Choose 4 distinct hash-random controls from the full panel first. Rank the entire panel by original normalized global wall peak (lower better). Among remaining entries use independent hash order to pick 4 from best fifth, middle fifth (40–60 percent) and worst fifth. All candidate IDs and assignments sealed before any new FIREFLY solve. Exclude cross-stratum duplicates within each source among the selected entries by the same material-coordinate threshold in stratum order; backfill before native solves. Abort if any arm cannot be filled.
4. Single active source matching each stratum. Both baseline source cases repeated. Fine common magnetic mesh, shared fixed equilibrium, reflecting midplane, 1 MW each independent source, nominal T=100 eV, n=1e19, effective transverse coefficient 0.45 m2/s, parallel step 2 m, cutoff 0.2 physical seconds. Physical wall discretization uses common 5 micrometre polyline approximation of canonically screened geometry, same closure and arclength origin. Retain fine existing mesh only if all walls clear its casing by 10 mm, otherwise build one enclosing 12 mm buffered common hull. Equal 4 MPI ranks, 1024 histories per run and three fresh seeds 32027,51749,83077 for every geometry. Interleave cases in hash order. Computational failure can be retried with same settings and no candidate replacement.
5. Primary endpoint is strict improvement of global toroidally averaged physical wall peak vs same-source baseline, pooling the 3 power profiles before taking maximum in conservative 2 mm rings, phase 0. Primary contrast is equal-weight stratum-average difference of candidate success fractions (top minus random). Peak minimum per four-candidate portfolio, head-to-head probabilities, middle/lower arms, A2, independent seed deletions, a 5 percent improvement threshold, coarser bins and half-bin offset are secondary. Candidate-level stratified bootstrap interval conditional on the three seeds and small sample, plus seed sensitivity separately. A positive observed difference alone is insufficient evidence of screening utility if the interval includes zero. Report native core/elapsed time if available; equal-history budget excludes sunk campaign cost. No claim of experimental validation or generalization to other backgrounds, sources jointly, or new optimization campaigns.
