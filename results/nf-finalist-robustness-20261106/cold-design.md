# Cold two-point donor test

Before computing the withholding contrast, fix three archived cubic1280 OMP cases (original wall, stage 18/R020 and stage 34/R050), the 1 MW operating point with χ⊥ = 0.3 m²/s, and the existing pre-redistribution Tt < 5 eV diagnostic. The threshold and the 1.702% source-power diagnostic were previously published. Include every donor. Reuse each donor's original first-hit center, width and prescribed power.

The primary endpoint is the change in the maximum toroidal-area-averaged wall heat flux over the 2 mm phase-zero material rings when every tagged donor is withheld. Deposit the untagged donors identically, book all withheld power separately, and do not renormalize. Report tagged flux at the full-model hottest ring, the largest tagged flux on any ring, both whole-wall maxima, deposited power, and effective area A₂ using each profile's own deposited-power numerator. Repeat on 1 and 4 mm rings. Check donor weights against the independent source quadrature table, the original native deposition cells against the saved profile, and the analysis rings against the archived rebin of the original native cells.

For donor-specific retained fractions between zero and one on tagged donors, every ring's deposited flux lies between the fully withheld and fully retained values. Bound the peak and A₂ without assuming that a partially retained distribution has intermediate A₂. Verify rank robustness by comparing the resulting intervals among geometries. This retrospective, one-way fixed-kernel attenuation diagnostic does not model changed locations or widths, feedback, radiation, or detachment. These tags are separate from negative magnetic labels in the pre-impact routing study.
