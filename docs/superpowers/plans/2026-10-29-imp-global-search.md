## Geometry and alternatives

Use two cubic displacement curves for IMP, retaining the original endpoints. Match the outer endpoints to the adjoining fixed straight surfaces. At their shared corner use one common oriented tangent. Following adversarial review, optimize over the wider shared forward cone with normalized dot products >=0.2 (about 57.5 to 168.5 degrees), rather than just the original 90 to 136 degree sector. This adds one dimension to the eight IMP displacement coefficients. A fixed bisector would unnecessarily restrict the search; moving the original joint would change the engineering endpoint assumption, so neither is chosen. Both curvature signs remain permitted. Apply the continuous absolute curvature limit to both IMP surfaces and all four OMP surfaces. Retain the OMP baseline lip clearance and 1.2 material length cap. Baseline is a comparison reference, never exempted into the feasible candidate population.
