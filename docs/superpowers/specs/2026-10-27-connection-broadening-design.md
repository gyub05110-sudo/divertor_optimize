# Connection-length-dependent deposition surrogate

## Authorized scope
Preserve the direct frozen-field model and its saved results. Add a deterministic, power-accounted reduced broadening model, optimize the same 24 wall coefficients, and cross-evaluate direct and broadened candidates for independent IMP/OMP sources at lambda_q = 1,2,3,5,10 mm. The user authorized plan, adversarial revision, implementation and comparison on 2026-10-27. This directory is not a Git repository; preserve existing production files using a pre-edit SHA256 inventory and additive modules.

## Model and alternatives
Selected: a flux-coordinate endpoint deposition surrogate. A wall-arclength blur has incorrect grazing/flux-expansion behavior. A field-line-bundle marching solver would require a new plasma/PFR mesh and material-aware intercell fluxes; it is deferred. The selected model is an exploratory sensitivity model, NOT a solved parallel/perpendicular transport equation, a calibrated Eich fit, particle transport, detachment or radiation model.

For each finite-power source tube i, retain prescribed power P_i and actual full-wall first hit. Integrate the full 3D length L_i = integral |B|/|Bp| d(l_p) along the cached poloidal path up to that hit, including partial final segment. No clipping of L. Midplane-to-first-hit length is used, not X-point-to-target length. The exactly separatrix witness has zero power and is never assigned L or broadened as a finite donor.

Use source-specific xi = sign(dpsi/dR * outward_R_sign) (psi-psi_sep)/abs(dpsi/dR at source separatrix). It has metres, increases outward at the source, and is flux-equivalent to upstream distance locally. Set Gaussian STANDARD DEVIATION sigma_i = sigma_ref (L_i/L_ref)^p in xi, with fixed user-visible L_ref = 10 m; default p=1 and optional p=0.5 sensitivity. sigma_ref is an effective phenomenological parameter, not a measured diffusivity; it is not Eich's S (which uses a different Gaussian convention). Proposed sensitivity values: 0.1, 0.25, 0.5 mm, plus exact direct dispatch at zero. The upstream lambda remains unchanged. These are assumptions to scan, not fit data or a claim of Super-X performance.

For monotone receiving cells a->b on the same connected monotone-xi wall branch as donor's actual hit, fractional power is abs(Phi((xi_b-xi_i)/sigma_i)-Phi((xi_a-xi_i)/sigma_i)). Surface density at quadrature points is sum_i P_i Gaussian(xi-xi_i) abs(dxi/ds)/(2*pi*R). This accounts for toroidal area and wall orientation once, without an extra sin(angle). Use whole-wall cells, including fixed portions and the continuous PFR side. The peak is sampled continuous density, not power divided by a coarse bin. Refinement must cover source rays, field tracing, receiving cells and peak points.

Support is additionally limited by the entire triangular fan from a fixed small distance (5 mm poloidal by default) upstream of the donor first hit to both endpoints of each straight receiver cell. The fan (the union of every chord to that cell) must lie inside the candidate vessel plasma domain and avoid LCFS/material solids; degenerate fans are lines. This supersedes the initial sparse-chord proposal after a thin-obstacle counterexample. It is a conservative LOCALITY safeguard, NOT proof of a physical cross-field diffusion trajectory. Blocked mass is explicit. Receiver cells split at every spline knot crossing and every real directional-derivative polynomial root within each patch, with disjoint-node residual checks. This supersedes sparse sign tests after a paired-hidden-root counterexample. No wraparound across a closed wall seam unless proven same monotone branch; ambiguous donor hits at branch boundaries fail closed.

## Conservation and feasibility
No kernel renormalization. Account for deposited, blocked, branch-tail and unhit power independently for each source width. Their sum must equal prescribed power (1) within 1e-10. Require unresolved total <=1e-3 for exploratory admissibility; report component fractions and affected tube counts. Retain full-wall geometry and explicit 1.5-degree shelf incidence constraint, applying it to newly receiving powered regions too. Direct zero-spread delegates exactly, including the old SOL-limit guard and objective. Nonzero spread keeps boundary geometry/incidence diagnostics but never mixes an unbroadened boundary heat witness into the new peak. Invalid field/length/kernel/geometric evidence must fail closed.

## Comparison and evidence
Use a separate runner and separate versioned result schema. Same source power, source support, lambda set, geometry freedoms and bounds for direct and surrogate. Include zero shape, available historical direct candidate, and separately searched direct/broadened candidates in cross-evaluation. Track shape displacement, per-width all-wall peak ratios, connection-length distribution, deposited/unresolved power and feasibility. Use deterministic Sobol+DE with fixed seeds; objective is piecewise smooth, not globally smooth. Report candidate comparisons, never global optimality.

Independent checks: analytic helical length including truncation; Gaussian CDF conservation and area Jacobian; blocked wall tests; pure Gaussian and exponential-convolution tests; direct dispatch equivalence; distinct IMP/OMP normalizations; near-separatrix resolution; independent full-wall first hits; all original production hashes preserved. Compare candidates on refined source and receiving grids and quintic field interpolation. Improvement acceptance requires feasibility at all levels, ratios stable within 0.002, no width worsened, and claimed improvement exceeding twice observed ratio variation + 0.0001. Failed screens remain failures in exported results.

Final reviewed numerical gates additionally require density-integrated power versus CDF power <=1e-4, independently reintegrated connection-length relative discrepancy <=0.005, path-flux drift/sigma <=0.05, and absolute peak refinement <=0.005. These are declared empirical screens, not rigorous bounds. All four audit levels including the coarsest must pass; original baseline/candidate failures cannot be erased by refinement. Anchor sensitivity (2.5/10 mm) and exponent sensitivity (0.5 versus 1) remain separately reported model assumptions. Results are exploratory and can fail these screens.

Primary model background: https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.04148 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.3246 . The surrogate is inspired by transport-dependent broadening but does not implement these papers' complete physical models.
