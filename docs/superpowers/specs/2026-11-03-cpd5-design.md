# CPD_5 fixed-equilibrium divertor optimization

The user requests a GitHub branch named CPD_5, the supplied CPD_5 geometry,
FreeGS equilibrium fixed before optimization, and a three-hour optimization
budget. Existing D3D studies remain immutable.

The user explicitly approved preserving available D3D plasma operating values
and thermal assumptions while solving CPD_5 PF/OH currents in FreeGS. They also
approved a simple pressure/current profile fitted to D3D Ip, reference toroidal
field and normalized beta, with a geometry-compatible lower-single-null target.
These are declared modeling assumptions, not experimental reconstruction.

Preserve raw input hashes. Interpret YAML arcs using their start, middle and end
points in metres. CPD_5 blanket and divertor are separate solids: do not invent
material bridges at gaps. Use every relevant actual material contour for first
intersection and power accounting. The STEP is a preserved CAD reference; the
axisymmetric node contours are the computational geometry.

Use D3D t=1400 ms Ip=1.3881240684837103 MA and betaN=0.6166043877601624.
Define reference Bt as the existing D3D vacuum field at its magnetic-axis radius,
and impose that Bt at CPD_5 nominal R0=4.007 m. Coil solutions are ampere-turns;
unknown winding counts do not imply hardware conductor currents. Fit pressure
before freezing; never retune equilibrium in response to optimized geometry.

The search reuses fixed field-line paths, toroidal connection integrals and the
existing conservative deposition model where its geometric assumptions apply.
IMP and OMP remain separate 1 MW sources with density 1e19 m^-3, chi_perp=0.3
m^2/s, lambda_q=3 mm, gamma=7, kappa0=2000. Compare baseline and candidates
at identical resolution, power and transport settings. Reject missing-power,
material-overlap and geometry failures explicitly. Report peak and footprint
spread along with power balance, runtime and numerical sensitivity.

Three hours starts only after a valid fixed equilibrium and baseline evaluator
are available. The bounded campaign includes search and final candidate
re-evaluation. Persist checkpoints and terminate at the deadline; an invalid
baseline prevents launching a misleading optimization. Report blockers rather
than claim an unperformed run or global optimality.
